This tool reproduces the population-level cluster-assignment procedure derived from the study's training cohort (DALI and GESTDIA). It is provided for research transparency and reproducibility alongside the manuscript and is not validated for individual clinical decision-making. Cluster centroids reflect the study population and have not been externally validated.

Reference implementation of Table 2 from the manuscript “Early pregnancy data-driven cluster phenotyping defines risk constellations for later gestational diabetes and adverse outcomes.” All computation runs client-side; no patient data is ever transmitted or stored. This page loads its fonts and the spreadsheet-reading library (SheetJS) from public CDNs, but requires no server or build step of its own. Provided as part of the study's code availability statement.
